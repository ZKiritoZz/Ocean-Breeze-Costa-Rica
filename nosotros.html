About Us

Family-owned private tourist transportation company in Guanacaste

Who We Are

Ocean Breeze Transport and Tourism is a family-owned private tourist transportation company based in Guanacaste, Costa Rica, led by Sergio de Jesús Arrieta Fonseca (ID 3-0438-0812).

We operate under the Special Stable Tourism Permit from the Public Transport Council (CTP) No. TUR-0045327, File TU-2073, valid from October 27, 2025 to September 30, 2027, authorized nationwide, and Costa Rican Tourism Board (ICT) Registration Code No. 4100-2025. Our authorized unit is minibus plate GB-003813, Ford Transit model 2020, capacity 15 passengers, with Dekra technical inspection and INS insurance up to date.

Our sloth hugging the palm tree logo is not a coincidence: the sloth was declared National Symbol of Costa Rica by Law 10007. It represents our philosophy: slow, safe travel, enjoying every detail, with respect for biodiversity.

Mission

To provide safe, punctual, premium private tourist transportation in Guanacaste and all Costa Rica, exceeding international traveler expectations with responsible driving, Pura Vida service and full compliance with Traffic Law 9078 and Law 7600.

Vision 2028

To be the reference tourist transport company in Guanacaste licensed with the Country Brand esencial Costa Rica, recognized for its commitment to sustainability, safety and Costa Rican identity.
